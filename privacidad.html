Privacy Policy — Stickman Workshop

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Español abajo

Stickman Workshop is an animation app. It has no online accounts, shows no ads and does not collect personal data.

What is stored and where

Your profiles, projects, drawings, audio, settings and the optional profile password are stored only on your computer, inside the app's storage. Passwords are stored hashed, never as plain text. None of it is sent to any server. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you press the button to record voice or sound. The recording stays inside your project, on your computer. It is not sent anywhere.

Animating with another person

If you use the feature to connect with another computer, the app connects directly to that computer (WebRTC). To find each other, both apps exchange a temporary connection code through the public service ntfy.sh; that code does not include your name or your projects. A Google STUN server is also contacted to find the connection route. This is only used if you start that feature.

Fonts

The app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time it opens with internet. Google may receive normal technical data from that download (such as the IP address).

Children

The app does not ask for an email, real name or any data to work. The profile name can be a nickname.

Contact

Política de privacidad — Stickman Workshop

Stickman Workshop es una app para hacer animaciones. No tiene cuentas en internet, no muestra anuncios y no recopila datos personales.

Tus perfiles, proyectos, dibujos, audios, ajustes y la clave opcional de cada perfil se guardan solo en tu computadora. Las claves se guardan cifradas (hash). Nada se envía a ningún servidor.

El micrófono solo se usa cuando tú pulsas grabar, y la grabación se queda en tu proyecto.

Si usas la función de animar con otra computadora, la app se conecta directo con ella (WebRTC), usando ntfy.sh para intercambiar un código temporal y un servidor STUN de Google para encontrar la ruta. El código no incluye tu nombre ni tus proyectos.

Las fuentes de letra se descargan de Google Fonts la primera vez. La app no pide correo, nombre real ni otros datos.
